# Engagement Guides

Practical, end-to-end guides for planning, conducting and delivering professional cybersecurity assessments.

These guides are designed to take an assessor from initial engagement preparation through reconnaissance, testing, validation, evidence collection and final reporting.

They complement the technical references throughout the Cybersecurity Field Manual.

## Engagement Lifecycle

Most assessments follow a similar lifecycle:

1. **Preparation** — establish objectives, scope, authorisation and rules of engagement.
2. **Discovery** — identify relevant assets, systems, applications or physical locations.
3. **Assessment** — systematically test the agreed attack surface.
4. **Validation** — verify potential findings and establish their actual impact.
5. **Evidence collection** — capture sufficient information to reproduce and substantiate findings.
6. **Reporting** — document findings, risk, business impact and remediation.
7. **Close-out** — communicate results, remove assessment artefacts and complete handover.

## Important Considerations

- Only assess systems, applications, networks and facilities covered by written authorisation.
- Follow the agreed scope and rules of engagement.
- Do not assume that access to one asset authorises testing of connected assets.
- Avoid unnecessary disruption, data exposure or changes to production systems.
- Use the minimum level of exploitation necessary to validate a finding.
- Protect collected evidence and sensitive information.
- Stop testing and notify the appropriate contact if an unexpected critical issue or operational impact occurs.
