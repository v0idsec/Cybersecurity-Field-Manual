## Purpose

Track corrective and preventive actions identified during incident response and post-incident reviews.

The register provides accountability and a way to verify that improvements have actually been implemented.

An action should not be marked complete simply because someone has performed a task. Completion should be supported by evidence and, where appropriate, independent validation.

## Action Priority

| Priority | Description | Suggested handling |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate risk reduction required | Escalate and address urgently |
| High | Significant security or operational weakness | Prioritise with an agreed short deadline |
| Medium | Meaningful improvement required | Schedule and track |
| Low | General improvement or maturity enhancement | Complete through normal planning |

The organisation should define its own priority criteria and target timeframes.

## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not Started | Action has been approved but work has not begun |
| In Progress | Work is underway |
| Blocked | Work cannot proceed due to a documented dependency |
| Complete | Work has been implemented and completion criteria met |
| Accepted Risk | An authorised person has formally accepted the residual risk |
| Cancelled | Action was formally withdrawn with a documented reason |

## Risk Acceptance

If an action cannot be completed, record:

- The reason it cannot be completed.
- The residual risk.
- Compensating controls.
- The person authorised to accept the risk.
- The date of acceptance.
- The next review date.

Risk acceptance must follow organisational policy and delegated authority.

## Closure Checklist

- [ ] All actions have accountable owners.
- [ ] Due dates have been agreed.
- [ ] Critical and high-priority actions have been escalated.
- [ ] Blocked actions have documented dependencies.
- [ ] Completion evidence has been recorded.
- [ ] Verification has been performed.
- [ ] Accepted risks have formal approval.
- [ ] Outstanding actions have been transferred to ongoing risk tracking where appropriate.
- [ ] The register has been reviewed and approved.
