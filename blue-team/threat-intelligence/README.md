## Objectives

- Understand relevant threats and adversary behaviour.
- Identify and validate indicators of compromise.
- Enrich suspicious infrastructure and artefacts.
- Support detection engineering and threat hunting.
- Inform incident response and risk management.
- Share intelligence responsibly.
