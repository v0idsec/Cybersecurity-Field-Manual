## 4. Exercise Boundaries

- No real credential collection.
- No collection of MFA codes.
- No malware delivery.
- No destructive actions.
- No unauthorised access to personal accounts.
- No real payment requests.
- No unauthorised impersonation of external parties.
- No contact with customers or suppliers unless explicitly approved.
- No activity outside the approved window.

## 7. Stop Conditions

Immediately stop the exercise if:

- The simulation affects production systems unexpectedly.
- A participant experiences significant distress.
- Real credentials or sensitive information are collected.
- An external party is contacted unexpectedly.
- The exercise exceeds authorised scope.
- Business operations are disrupted.
- The approver requests termination.
