## Subject

Security notification: Suspicious email identified

## Message

Hello,

Our security team has identified a suspicious email that may have been delivered to your mailbox.

The message may contain misleading links, unexpected attachments or requests for sensitive information.

**Please take the following actions:**

- Do not click links in the message.
- Do not open or forward suspicious attachments.
- Do not reply to the sender.
- Do not provide passwords, MFA codes or financial information.
- Report the message using the organisation's approved reporting process.

If you have already interacted with the message, please contact the IT or security team immediately.

Please let us know if you:
- Clicked a link.
- Entered your credentials.
- Approved an unexpected MFA request.
- Downloaded or opened an attachment.
- Replied to the sender.
- Disclosed confidential information.
- Made or approved a payment.

There is no need to feel embarrassed if you interacted with the message. Early reporting helps us respond quickly and protect the organisation.
