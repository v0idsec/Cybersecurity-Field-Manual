## Communication Safeguards

Before publication:

- Verify all facts.
- Obtain appropriate approvals.
- Avoid identifying affected employees unnecessarily.
- Avoid exposing sensitive investigation details.
- Provide clear instructions.
- Ensure reporting channels are operational.
- Use accessible language.
