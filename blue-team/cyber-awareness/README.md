## Core Principles

- Make security understandable and relevant.
- Encourage reporting without fear of blame.
- Focus on behaviour and practical outcomes.
- Tailor education to roles and responsibilities.
- Reinforce learning regularly.
- Measure meaningful improvements rather than attendance alone.
- Respect privacy and applicable employment requirements.
