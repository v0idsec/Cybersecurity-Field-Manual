# Instagram Account Recovery

**Think someone has accessed or taken over your Instagram account?**

An attacker may change your username, email address or password, send messages to your followers, post scam content or try to demand money for returning your account.

Do not pay anyone claiming they can guarantee recovery.

## Step 1. Use Instagram's official recovery page

Go to:

[Instagram Hacked Account Recovery](https://www.instagram.com/hacked/)

Choose the option that best describes your situation and follow the instructions.

If you receive an email saying your account details were changed, check whether Instagram provides an option to reverse the change.

Do not trust links sent by strangers claiming to be Instagram support.

## Step 2. Try to regain access

Use the Instagram app or official website.

You may be able to request a login link or security code using your username, email address or phone number.

If those details have been changed, continue through the hacked account recovery process.

## Step 3. Change your password

Once you regain access:

1. Open Instagram.
2. Go to your profile.
3. Open Settings and activity.
4. Open Accounts Centre.
5. Select Password and security.
6. Change your password.

Use a unique password.

## Step 4. Check active sessions

In Accounts Centre:

1. Open Password and security.
2. Select Where you're logged in.
3. Review devices and locations.
4. Log out anything you do not recognise.

## Step 5. Check account details

Review:

- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Username.
- Connected Facebook account.
- Two-factor authentication methods.
- Accounts Centre settings.

Remove unfamiliar information and restore your own recovery details.

## Step 6. Check messages and posts

Look for:

- Messages you did not send.
- Posts or stories you did not publish.
- Unfamiliar accounts being followed.
- Changed profile information.
- Scam links or cryptocurrency promotions.
- Unusual advertising activity.

Delete fraudulent content where possible and warn affected followers.

## Step 7. Turn on two-factor authentication

In Accounts Centre:

1. Open Password and security.
2. Select Two-factor authentication.
3. Choose your Instagram account.
4. Follow the setup instructions.

Use an authenticator app or another supported method.

## Step 8. Check connected apps

Review third-party apps and websites that have access to your account.

Remove anything unfamiliar or unnecessary.

Be especially cautious of follower-tracking applications, unofficial account tools and services promising extra engagement.

## If you cannot recover the account

Use:

[Instagram Hacked Account Recovery](https://www.instagram.com/hacked/)

Follow the official identity verification process if offered.

Instagram may ask you to confirm that you own the account.
