# WhatsApp Account Recovery

**Think someone is trying to take over your WhatsApp account?**

WhatsApp accounts are linked to phone numbers. Criminals may try to register your number on another device, trick you into sharing a verification code or convince you to scan a QR code that links their device to your account.

## Important: Never share your WhatsApp verification code

If you receive a WhatsApp verification code that you did not request:

- Do not share it with anyone.
- Do not send a screenshot of it.
- Do not read it to someone claiming to be support.
- Do not approve a login request you did not initiate.

Receiving an unexpected code does not automatically mean someone has accessed your account. It may mean someone has attempted to register your number.

## Step 1. If you can still access WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp on your phone.

Check:

1. Open Settings.
2. Select Linked devices.
3. Review every linked device.
4. Log out any device you do not recognise.

If someone has linked their browser or computer to your account, removing that device helps stop their access.

## Step 2. If someone has taken over your account

If you have been logged out and believe someone else registered your number:

1. Open the official WhatsApp application.
2. Enter your phone number.
3. Request the registration code by SMS or phone call.
4. Enter the code you receive.

Registering your number again should log out the person using your account on another phone.

Never give this code to another person.

## Step 3. If WhatsApp asks for a two-step verification PIN

If the attacker enabled a two-step verification PIN, you may be asked to enter it.

If you do not know the PIN, follow WhatsApp's official recovery instructions.

You may need to wait before you can regain access.

Do not pay someone who claims they can bypass this process.

## Step 4. Check your phone number

If you suddenly cannot receive calls or SMS messages, your mobile number may also have been compromised.

Contact your mobile provider immediately.

See [SIM Swap & Phone Number Recovery](sim-swap-and-phone-number-recovery.md).

## Step 5. Warn your contacts

Contact friends and family using another method.

Tell them:

- Your WhatsApp account may have been compromised.
- Ignore unusual requests for money.
- Do not share verification codes.
- Do not open unexpected links sent from your account.

Scammers often impersonate someone and ask their contacts to urgently transfer money.

## Step 6. Turn on two-step verification

Once you regain access:

1. Open WhatsApp Settings.
2. Select Account.
3. Select Two-step verification.
4. Enable it.
5. Set a PIN that other people cannot easily guess.
6. Add an email address for recovery if offered.

Keep your recovery email account secure as well.

## Step 7. Check your account again

Review:

- Linked devices.
- Profile information.
- Group memberships.
- Messages you did not send.
- Unusual activity reported by contacts.

Remove unfamiliar linked devices and leave groups you did not join.
