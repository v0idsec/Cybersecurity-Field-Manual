# Facebook Account Recovery

**Think someone has accessed or taken over your Facebook account?**

A compromised Facebook account can be used to impersonate you, scam your friends, access private messages or advertise fraudulent products.

Start with Facebook's official recovery page.

## Step 1. Try Facebook's recovery process

Go to:

[Facebook Hacked Account Recovery](https://www.facebook.com/hacked)

Follow the instructions to identify your account and recover access.

If you can still sign in, continue with the security checks below.

## Step 2. Change your password

Once you regain access:

1. Open Facebook.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Open Accounts Centre.
4. Select Password and security.
5. Change your password.

Choose a unique password that you have not used on other websites.

## Step 3. Check where you're logged in

In Accounts Centre:

1. Open Password and security.
2. Select Where you're logged in.
3. Review the devices and locations.
4. Log out sessions you do not recognise.

If you're unsure, log out of all sessions and sign back in yourself.

## Step 4. Check your contact information

Review the email addresses and phone numbers linked to your account.

Remove anything you do not recognise.

Make sure your own email address and phone number are still available for recovery.

## Step 5. Check account changes

Review:

- Recent posts and comments.
- Messages you did not send.
- Pages you do not recognise.
- Advertising accounts.
- Payment methods.
- Connected applications.
- Friends or groups added without your permission.

Remove unfamiliar access and report fraudulent activity.

## Step 6. Turn on two-factor authentication

In Accounts Centre:

1. Open Password and security.
2. Select Two-factor authentication.
3. Choose your account.
4. Follow the setup instructions.

Use an authenticator app or another supported method where possible.

Do not share login codes with anyone.

## Step 7. Warn your friends

If your account sent scam messages or requests for money, contact your friends through another channel.

Tell them:

- Your account was compromised.
- They should ignore suspicious messages.
- They should not send money.
- They should not open unexpected links from the account.

## Step 8. Check for linked account problems

If your Facebook and Instagram accounts are connected through Accounts Centre, review both.

Secure any other account that may have shared the same password.

## If you cannot recover your account

Use:

[Facebook Hacked Account Recovery](https://www.facebook.com/hacked)

If your email account was also compromised, secure that first or alongside Facebook recovery.
