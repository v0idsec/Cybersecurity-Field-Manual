# Account Security & Recovery

Practical recovery guides for people who suspect an online account, phone number or device has been compromised.

These guides are designed to be shared directly with customers, colleagues, friends and family. You do not need a technical background to follow them.

## Important

These guides provide general guidance, not a guarantee that an account or device is safe.

If money has been stolen, contact the bank immediately. If identity documents or personal information have been exposed, consider contacting IDCARE. If a work account or device is involved, contact your organisation's IT or security team.

You are not stupid for getting caught by a scam. These attacks are designed to trick people.
